About this model

Body, doors, glass, wheels and cabin: 2021 Volkswagen Golf GTI by Ddiaz Design (Sketchfab), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial use only.

The DGEA engine and the front corners are modelled from photos of removed Golf 8 / A3 8Y 1.4 eHybrid parts, with educated measurements scaled to this car. Every constructed shape and socket size is an estimate, not OEM CAD.

Torque, angle and renew figures come from the VW workshop manual assembly overviews for the Golf Mk7 (same EA211 engine family, MQB front axle and brakes). Where the data has no figure, the part says so. OEM part numbers are not included.

Published VW figures: 1.4 TSI EA211, 1,395 cc, bore 74.5 mm, stroke 80.0 mm; e-motor 85 kW; 13 kWh battery; 40 L tank.

Studio light: Studio Small 09 HDRI by Sergej Majboroda, Poly Haven, CC0. Photos: three-gpu-pathtracer and three-mesh-bvh by Garrett Johnson, MIT.
