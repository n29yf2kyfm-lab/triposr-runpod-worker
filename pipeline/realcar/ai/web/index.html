Vehicle AI

The trained vehicle model, running on this phone. It reads a photo (car, van or motorbike, the way it points, its paint) and checks a 3D build against its photo. Your pictures stay on the phone.

The model (90 MB) downloads the first time you check a picture, then stays on this phone.

How good it is
What it can't do
  • It is a second opinion, not a review. The shape check sees whether a build looks like the photo's model; it doesn't see a wrong door count, a roof colour or a broken part.
  • It can't tell a vehicle from no vehicle: a wheel close-up still gets a kind, a side and a colour.
  • Side is the way a side or three-quarter view points. A photo from straight ahead or behind has no answer worth reading. On the phone it reads side a little worse than the full model on a computer (91 photos in 100 against 95): the phone's copy of the image model is the 90 MB one.
  • Colours it confuses most: silver with white, grey with dark grey.
  • It learned from one run of 936 vehicles, labelled by agents with checked items. A label is sometimes wrong, and so is an answer.
